About Kitaably

Kitaably is an independent novel discovery and review website, created for readers who want to find their next great book without spending hours searching.

What we focus on

We keep our attention on a few things and try to do them well: simple reviews, spoiler-free recommendations, a mix of genres, and a starting point for readers who are new to a genre or just looking for their next book.

Our Goal — to make discovering your next book simple, enjoyable and less overwhelming.

What You Can Expect

  • Honest opinions, written plainly
  • Easy-to-read reviews without unnecessary jargon
  • Genre-based recommendations, from classics to thrillers
  • Suggestions for different types of readers, not just one kind
